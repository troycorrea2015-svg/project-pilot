3) VERCEL ENVIRONMENT VARIABLES
-------------------------------
Use VERCEL_ENVIRONMENT_VALUES.txt as the checklist.
The paid Permit Concierge requires:
- NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL=https://www.projectpiloting.com
- Supabase URL, publishable/anon key, and service-role secret
- STRIPE_SECRET_KEY
- STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET
- PERMIT_SERVICE_PAYMENTS_ENABLED=true
- PERMIT_CONCIERGE_PRICE_CENTS=19900
- NEXT_PUBLIC_PERMIT_CONCIERGE_PRICE_CENTS=19900

Required before accepting paid Permit Concierge orders:
- OPENAI_API_KEY
- RESEND_API_KEY
- MARKETPLACE_FROM_EMAIL (must be a sender/domain verified with your email provider)
- PERMIT_CONCIERGE_EMAIL (a real inbox you will monitor)

Optional but recommended:
- NEXT_PUBLIC_GOOGLE_MAPS_EMBED_API_KEY

4) STRIPE WEBHOOK
-----------------
Endpoint:
https://www.projectpiloting.com/api/marketplace/webhook

Subscribe to:
- checkout.session.completed
- checkout.session.expired

The same webhook handles both Permit Concierge purchases and contractor introduction purchases.

5) TEST BEFORE LIVE MONEY
-------------------------
Use Stripe TEST keys first.
A) Create a homeowner account.
B) Create a project.
C) Open the permit area and start Full-Service Permit Concierge.
D) Complete a Stripe TEST checkout.
E) Confirm the project shows the Permit Concierge as active only after payment.
F) In Admin, confirm the order appears in Financials and the Permit operations queue.

H) Test an intake-stage refund once.

6) TURN ON REAL REVENUE
-----------------------
After the test passes:
A) Replace STRIPE_SECRET_KEY with the live sk_live_ value.
B) Create/update the LIVE Stripe webhook and replace STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET with its live endpoint secret.
C) Keep PERMIT_SERVICE_PAYMENTS_ENABLED=true.
D) Deploy again.
E) Open /admin/launch-readiness. It should show the critical revenue checks ready.

MARKETPLACE_PAYMENTS_ENABLED can remain false until you are ready to charge contractors for qualified introductions. The $199 Permit Concierge service can earn revenue independently.

7) ACTUALLY OPERATE PAID CASES
------------------------------
A purchase is not passive fulfillment. When a customer pays, Project Pilot creates a paid case and work queue. Someone operating Project Pilot must verify the jurisdiction and official filing route, prepare/coordinate the authorized work, communicate with the customer, and move the case through closeout.

Do not claim Project Pilot can perform a step that the government reserves for the applicant, property owner, licensed contractor, architect, engineer, notary, or other authorized professional.
