# Project Pilot 4.5 Permit Concierge operations standard

## Customer-experience rule
Every active case should answer four questions without making the homeowner interpret permit terminology:

1. What is Project Pilot doing?
2. What does the homeowner need to do?
3. What is the next checkpoint?
4. What will trigger the next update?

If the Customer Experience Preview in the admin workbench does not answer those clearly, update the status, summary, or tasks before leaving the case.

## Do not invent timelines
For government review, do not publish a made-up approval estimate. Use an event-based expectation such as “Next update when the permit authority posts a review result or requests changes.” Only show a date when a real task, correction response, inspection, or authority deadline has a saved date.

## Homeowner tasks
Create a homeowner task only for something Project Pilot cannot legitimately complete for the applicant, such as a required personal signature, identity verification, applicant-only portal action, government payment, professional document, or other controlled requirement.

A homeowner task should be written in plain language and explain exactly what to do. When complete, the workflow should resume without making the homeowner re-navigate the process.

## Project Pilot tasks
Keep one Project Pilot task in progress whenever possible. Completing a queue item should advance the next task and synchronize the customer-facing permit status.

## Customer updates
Use meaningful status events. When outbound email is configured, major status changes, customer-action requests, corrections, coordinator messages, and important inspection results can also notify the customer by email. The in-app permit record remains the source of truth.

## Official sources
Record the responsible authority and official permit starting point before relying on jurisdiction-specific requirements. Do not describe a generic checklist as a verified government requirement.

## Closeout
A permit is not Complete until the actual permit case is closed. Approval alone is not completion when inspections or closeout remain.
